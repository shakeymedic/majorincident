MITT — Major Incident Triage Tool: quick reference

majorincident.netlify.app • works without signal once installed • print this for the kit bag

Before an incident

  1. Open the app once with signal. Wait for ✓ Ready offline in the blue bar.
  2. iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: tap Install (or Chrome menu → Install app). Always open it from the home-screen icon.
  3. iPhone: set Auto-Lock to Never while on scene.
  4. Practise in Training mode (⚙ → Training mode). Orange banner = practice only; real records are untouched.

Start

  1. Type your name / call sign.
  2. Tap your role (healthcare professional, or non-clinical).
  3. Optional: under Options, type the incident code — the same on every phone.

Triage a patient

  1. Tap START TRIAGE. Answer YES / NO.
  2. Not sure? Follow the yellow hint — it gives the safer answer.
  3. Read the category and attach the matching label.
  4. Tapped the wrong button? ↩ Change last answer.
  5. Next Patient → for the next casualty.

Hand over one patient (two phones)

  1. Sender: open the patient → 📱 QR Handover.
  2. Receiver: 📷 Receive patient → scan → check → Accept.
  3. Receiver's phone shows a confirmation code.
  4. Sender: 📷 Scan their confirmation code. Done — both phones record it.

Send many patients

  1. More → Send patients to another device → choose all / sector / on scene.
  2. Show QR codes. Receiver: 📷 Receive patient and keep the camera on the codes (any order).
  3. No luck scanning? Export transfer file and send it by AirDrop / Nearby Share; receiver uses Import transfer file.

Records for debrief and inquiry

  • 📋 Patient log → Casualty Register (CSV), Audit Trail (CSV), Full Incident Archive (JSON).
  • Verify audit trail shows a head hash — photograph it.
  • Close / Reset incident needs a saved archive first.

If something goes wrong

  • Red Saving failed banner → tap Export archive now, then keep working.
  • Scanning is slow → screen brightness up, phones 20–30 cm apart, hold still; or use a transfer file.
  • "This is a TRAINING code" / "This is a REAL patient code" → one phone is in training mode. Switch it in ⚙.

Categories

P1 — Immediate
P2 — Urgent
P3 — Delayed
Not breathing (TST) — healthcare reassessment ASAP
Dead (MITT)
P1 Hold — senior clinical decision

Question wording follows the NHS England TST and NHS MITT cards (April 2023). The app supports, and does not replace, training and clinical judgement.
