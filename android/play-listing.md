## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Major Incident Triage helps trained responders practise NHS England Ten Second Triage and the Major Incident Triage Tool, record interventions and hand casualties over between phones in exercises.

Training and exercise use only. Not for use with real patients. This app is not a medical device and has not been approved for clinical use.

What you can practise:
• Ten Second Triage (TST) for any responder, and the Major Incident Triage Tool (MITT) for clinicians at the casualty clearing station.
• Recording interventions, injuries and notes for each casualty, with the time of each triage.
• Handing casualties over to another phone by QR code, and receiving them.
• A patient log and a map of casualty positions.
• A METHANE report.
• A commander screen for a laptop or big screen that shows every phone's casualties, counts by priority, alerts and the hospital board, during a live exercise.

Built for exercises:
• A demo incident with made-up casualties, for showing the app.
• A live exercise mode with control room, field triager and casualty clearing roles on separate phones.
• Triage works with no signal. Live sharing with a commander screen needs signal and is protected by a code and PIN.

No accounts and no adverts. Live sharing sends triage records (no patient names or NHS numbers are asked for), and the server copy is deleted 24 hours after the last update.

Published by West Midlands Evidence Based Emergency Medicine Ltd (EM Evidence). Not produced or endorsed by NHS England.
```
