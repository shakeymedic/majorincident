Running a live exercise

Before you start

  • Every device needs internet. Install the app on each phone.
  • Pick a control room device and a big screen (laptop, TV or tablet) for the commander screen.
  • Brief your casualties (actors or cards): can they walk, are they breathing, catastrophic bleeding, breathing rate, pulse or capillary refill. The app asks the TST and MITT questions in order.
  • Agree how teams talk to each other, as on the day: radio, phone or runner. The app does not replace this.

Set up (about 2 minutes)

  1. Control room: ▶ Demo → 🎯 Run a live exercise → 🎖 Control room. You get a code, PIN and QR. Tap Open the commander screen, or on the big screen open the link and type the PIN.
  2. Everyone else: type your name on the start page, then ▶ Demo → 🎯 Run a live exercise → your role, then scan the control room's QR.
  3. Check the commander screen lists every phone under Phones.

During the exercise

  • Field triagers triage each casualty, record bleeding control and position, then hand over to the CCZ: 📤 QR Handover on the patient, the CCZ phone scans it with 📷 Receive patient.
  • CCZ accepts each patient (the commander screen then shows them as at the CCS), re-triages with MITT, records treatment, then marks them evacuated and picks the receiving hospital.
  • Control room runs the incident from the commander screen alone: counts split into field, CCS and evacuated, the map, the needs-attention list, alerts with a sound (new P1, deterioration, tourniquet past an hour, phone gone quiet), the timeline, METHANE from the phones, the hospital board with each hospital's agreed capacity, phones, and every patient (tap any patient for their full details). Use the wall display on a TV. The view-only PIN lets an observer or a receiving hospital watch without being able to change anything.
  • Ideas for injects: a casualty deteriorates (tell the triager to re-triage), a new casualty appears late, a phone loses signal (aeroplane mode for 2 minutes, then it catches up), a tourniquet passes 1 hour.
  • If the commander screen says the server is unreachable, carry on: every phone keeps its own records and sends them when the link returns. For an urgent picture, a phone can use Home → More → Send patients to another device to show its patients as QR codes for the control room's phone to scan.

Finish and debrief

  • Control room: 🖨 Print summary on the commander screen (or save it as a PDF) and download the CSV for the debrief, then ⚙ Settings → End incident for everyone.
  • Everyone: tap ↩ Start page in the orange bar to go back to normal use. Practice records stay separate from real ones.
